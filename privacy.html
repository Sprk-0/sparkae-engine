Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026
This policy describes how the SparkAE marketing site and the SparkAE software handle data. It is provided as a clear-language summary and should be reviewed by your counsel before it governs a commercial agreement.

The short version

SparkAE is designed so that your assessment data stays inside your deployment boundary. In the default deterministic mode (LLM_PROVIDER=none) the engine makes zero outbound network calls — no telemetry, no auto-update, no calls home. Authorization packages, SSPs, evidence, and findings are processed entirely within the container you run. We never see them.

What this site collects

The marketing site is static and loads no third-party CDNs, fonts, analytics, or trackers. We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The only personal data we receive is what you choose to send us by email (for example, when you click “Start a Pilot” or “Contact Sales,” which open a pre-addressed message to our info@sparkae.ai inbox).

Information you send us

When you email us about a pilot, a partnership, or an integration we receive:

  • Your name, email address, and anything you include in the message.
  • Your organization and role, if you provide them.

We use this solely to respond to you and to administer a pilot or commercial relationship. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Assessment data inside the software

When you run the SparkAE software, the data you assess lives in your database within your infrastructure under multi-tenant isolation. Optional integrations — cloud LLM, SSO, webhooks, email, SIEM, and cloud storage — are off by default and only transmit data once you explicitly configure them. Choosing the cloud LLM mode sends content you submit to the configured provider; the default and local-LLM modes do not.

Retention

We retain pilot and sales correspondence for as long as needed to support the relationship and to meet legal obligations, then delete it. Data inside your SparkAE deployment is retained according to your own configuration and policies, not ours.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete the correspondence you’ve sent by emailing info@sparkae.ai.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@sparkae.ai.
