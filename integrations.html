Already Have a GRC Tool?
Good. Here's why you still need SparkAE.
Q. "We already have Drata/Vanta for compliance."
Drata monitors your controls. SparkAE assesses your SSP. They tell you if your MFA policy drifted. We tell you if your SSP's description of MFA is evidence-grounded on the Rev5 Examine pass. Different questions, different tools. Export CSV/JSON/OSCAL; you import. Not a certified two-way Drata sync.
Q. "We have ServiceNow GRC — we don't want another dashboard."
Then don't use our dashboard. Use export or the REST API. SparkAE runs the Rev5 Examine engine — the same engine as the 3PAO UI — and returns structured findings. Wiring those into ServiceNow is your integration, not a certified first-class connector.
Q. "Our GRC tool already has FedRAMP templates."
Templates help you write the SSP. They don't assess it. Your GRC tool gives you a template with 323 empty Moderate control boxes and 1,307 DIFs. SparkAE tells you whether what you wrote in those boxes will survive a 3PAO assessment. The template is the form. We're the red pen.
Q. "We can't justify another line item in the security budget."
Frame it differently: SparkAE is Examine-pass preparation on the Rev5 local engine — not a guaranteed savings number. Do not quote $40K–$80K. Hosted SaaS as a CSP SKU is not sold. Pilot the local engine; keep the gap report either way.
Q. "What if our GRC vendor adds this feature?"
They won't — and structurally can't. If Drata assesses the SSP that Drata helped you write, that's a conflict of interest. If Paramify assesses the OSCAL package that Paramify generated, same problem. SparkAE's value is independence. Your GRC vendor can't be independent from their own output. We can.
