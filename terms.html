What SparkAE is — and is not

SparkAE is an independent pre-assessment engine. It evaluates an authorization package against NIST SP 800-53A determination statements and returns Satisfied / Other-Than-Satisfied findings with evidence citations, confidence, and defensibility grades.

SparkAE is not a Third Party Assessment Organization (3PAO), and it does not issue the official Security Assessment Report (SAR) or an Authorization to Operate (ATO). Its findings are intended to support readiness and the EXAMINE pass — not to replace the independent assessment a certified 3PAO performs or the decision an authorizing official makes. You remain responsible for your own compliance determinations.
